Who We Are

Sun Shine Club, Kendupalli is a community-based youth organization committed to serving society through cultural celebrations, social awareness programs, educational initiatives, and community development.

Our members work together with dedication, teamwork, and compassion to create a positive environment for everyone while preserving our traditions and promoting harmony among villagers.

Celebrated Festivals

Every year our club organizes these festivals with great enthusiasm and active participation from all members.

Ganesh Puja

Ganesh Puja is celebrated with devotion, cultural programs, community gatherings, and prayers seeking Lord Ganesha's blessings for wisdom, prosperity, and success.

View Gallery

Saraswati Puja

Saraswati Puja is celebrated to honor Goddess Saraswati, the goddess of knowledge, wisdom, learning, and arts, with prayers, cultural events, and community participation.
