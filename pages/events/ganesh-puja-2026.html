Ganesh Puja

Ganesh Puja Celebration 2026

14 September 2026 08:00 AM Kendupalli, Bhapur, Nayagarh

On 14 September 2026, Sun Shine Club, Kendupalli will celebrate Ganesh Puja with members, families and the local community in an atmosphere of devotion, unity and cultural spirit.

Ganesh Puja Celebration

Sun Shine Club, Kendupalli is organizing Ganesh Puja to celebrate Lord Ganesha and bring members of the community together.

The celebration will provide an opportunity for club members, families and local residents to participate in religious and cultural activities and spend meaningful time together.

Community Celebration

Festivals play an important role in bringing communities together. Through Ganesh Puja, Sun Shine Club aims to encourage unity, cooperation and participation among residents of Kendupalli.

Members of the club will work together to make the celebration memorable while preserving the cultural traditions of the community.
