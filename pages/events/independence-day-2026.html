Independence Day

Independence Day Celebration & Biscuit Distribution 2026

15 August 2026 Kendupalli, Bhapur, Nayagarh

On 15 August 2026, Sun Shine Club, Kendupalli, joined the village community in celebrating India's Independence Day with a patriotic village parade.

Independence Day Celebration

The celebration brought together members of the community and school students in the spirit of patriotism, unity and respect for the nation.

During the village parade, school students participated in the Independence Day celebration. As part of the club's community initiative, Sun Shine Club distributed biscuits to the participating school students.

Biscuit Distribution to School Students

The biscuit distribution was organized as a small gesture of appreciation and encouragement for the school students participating in the village parade.
