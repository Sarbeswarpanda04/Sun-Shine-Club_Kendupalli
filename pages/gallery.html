About Us

Sun Shine Club is a community organization dedicated to promoting wellness, fitness, and social engagement in Kendupalli. We offer a variety of activities and events for members of all ages.

Our Activities

  • Yoga and Meditation Classes
  • Sports Tournaments
  • Community Service Projects
  • Workshops and Seminars

Join Us

If you're interested in becoming a member of Sun Shine Club, please contact us at info@sunshineclub-kendupalli.org.
