About Sun Shine Club

A youth-driven community organization dedicated to promoting unity, culture, social responsibility, and service in Kendupalli.

Who We Are

Sun Shine Club, Kendupalli is a community-based youth organization committed to serving society through cultural celebrations, social awareness programs, educational initiatives, and community development.

Our members work together with dedication, teamwork, and compassion to create a positive environment for everyone while preserving our traditions and promoting harmony among villagers.

Our Mission

To inspire young people to contribute towards society through service, cultural activities, education, and community welfare.

Our Vision

To build a united, educated, responsible, and progressive community where everyone works together for a brighter future.

What We Do

Ganesh Puja

Celebrating Lord Ganesha with devotion, unity and cultural programs.

Saraswati Puja

Honouring Goddess Saraswati and promoting education among youth.

Social Service

Participating in community welfare and social awareness activities.

Youth Development

Encouraging leadership, teamwork, discipline and responsibility.
